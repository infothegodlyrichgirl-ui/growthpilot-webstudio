Product overview

A simpler way to build money habits

Many people want to save with intention but lack simple tools and clear guidance. Tophora is being designed to bring savings goals, a clear view of progress and practical financial education together in one place.

It is being developed for African markets, with everyday users in mind, and is built by the GrowthPilot Digital Solutions team.
