FAQ

Pricing questions

Do I pay the full amount upfront?

No. Our standard arrangement is 50% before work begins and 50% before final handover or launch. Larger projects may use milestone payments.

Are domain and hosting included?

Not automatically. Domain registration and hosting are quoted separately unless specifically included in your package.

Can I request a custom website?

Yes. We can discuss your requirements and prepare a custom quotation.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We offer website redesign and improvement services.

How long does website development take?

Delivery time depends on the project scope, content readiness and required functionality. An estimated schedule is provided with your quotation.

Can I upgrade my website later?

Yes. Additional pages, features and integrations can be discussed as your business grows.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes. We offer monthly maintenance options with agreed service limits.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. GrowthPilot Digital Solutions welcomes enquiries from businesses in Uganda, across Africa and internationally. International quotations can be provided in an agreed currency.
