A word from the founder

Technology that is simple, dependable and easy to use.

I run GrowthPilot Digital Solutions, a small technology company working from Kampala, Uganda. I build websites and applications for businesses that want technology that is simple, dependable and easy to use.

Most of what I know came from doing the work: taking an idea, building a first version, seeing where it falls short and improving it. I use modern tools, including AI, to move faster, and I review everything myself before it reaches a client.

I care about the reasoning behind a decision, not only whether the page loads. Why this layout, why this tool, why not the simpler option. A good website or app should be easy to explain to the person who owns it.

Alongside client work, I am building my own products, starting with Tophora, a fintech platform in development for African markets. It is early, and I would rather be honest about that than overpromise.

If you have an idea, a problem or a project you are not sure how to start, tell me about it. We will work out a sensible first step together.
